02 / Methodology

Our Step-by-Step Repair Process

1

Inspection & Tracing

Water rarely enters directly above the drip. We trace water marks along rafters, insulation, and sheathing to find the true entry point.

2

Emergency Tarping

For active weather or severe storm damage, we immediately install heavy-duty waterproof tarping to protect your interior.

3

Decking Restoration

We strip down to the wood sheathing. Rotted or compromised plywood is removed and replaced with new exterior-grade OSB/plywood.

4

Moisture Barrier

We apply self-adhering ice and water protection over vulnerable points (valleys, eaves), followed by premium synthetic underlayment.

5

Flashing & Sealing

We interlock new corrosion-resistant step flashing and valley linings, sealing vent stacks and skylights with commercial elastomeric sealant.

6

Primary Material

New shingles, metal panels, or tiles are securely fastened to exact manufacturer specs, ensuring a seamless color and profile match.

7

Cleansite Guarantee

We execute magnetic sweeps for stray nails, clear gutters, and perform a multi-point quality check to guarantee watertight performance.

03 / Care

Preventative Maintenance Advice

Regular maintenance extends a roof's lifespan and prevents costly structural damage. We recommend property owners follow these essential steps:

Clean gutters and downspouts twice per year (spring and autumn) to allow proper runoff.

Trim back overhanging tree branches to reduce moss growth, debris accumulation, and physical impact risk.

Inspect attic spaces after heavy rains or snowstorms for signs of dampness, moisture odors, or daylight leaks.

Schedule a professional roof inspection at least once every 1-2 years and after severe wind or hail storms.
